Abstract
We evaluate causal probabilistic forecasts and capital-constrained local execution using
22 equities and ETFs, preserved historical snapshots, controlled chronological refits and a
separately frozen prospective collector. The saved experiment supplies 15,092 instrument-
date decisions from 726 rolling refits. In its consumed recent audit, the calibrated mixture
has 37.59% accuracy and 1.0882 log loss, compared with 40.62% and 1.0756 for the trailing
prior. Its paired log-loss disadvantage is 0.0126, with a date-block 95% interval [-0.0025,
0.0269]. Fresh-capital audit accounts return -0.46% for the adaptive hybrid and 2.55%
for the matched equal-weight control after costs. The follow-up executes 848 registered
training trials, with feature, calibration, adaptation and held-instrument comparisons. The
product includes a sealed local collector, persistent ledgers and a session-aware schedule;
engineering evidence is scoped separately from trading superiority. These experiments do
not establish a defensible deployable edge. Historical analyses are exploratory, prospective
sections remain interim, and external broker orders remain disabled.

dependent-sample inference; paper trading; reproducibility.

1 Motivation and research questions
A trading research system must answer two questions that are easy to confuse: whether its
calculations represent the stated strategy, and whether that strategy has useful economic
performance. Correct accounting is necessary for the second question, but it cannot answer it.
Likewise, a profitable curve cannot validate data that were unavailable at the simulated decision
time. This study follows the entire chain from public observations to predictions, constrained
targets, simulated fills, persisted account state and research claims. The output is an executable
local paper-trading product and a versioned empirical record. No external broker execution is
part of the experiment.
The starting repository contained classifiers, a strategy zoo, an ensemble, a dashboard and
saved backtests. Earlier audits identified accounting and timing defects. Subsequent repairs
produced a sealed adaptive experiment and a short technical report. We preserve that experiment
and examine its full prediction and ledger records instead of inferring behavior from headline
accuracy or isolated screenshots. This follow-up adds class and instrument diagnostics, controlled
refits, transfer tests, execution sensitivities and operational evidence. Previously inspected
historical observations are explicitly exploratory. A new analysis of an old holdout does not
restore its independence.
Our first question is whether causal forecasts improve probability quality and directional
classification over a trailing class-frequency estimate and simple forecasting controls. The primary
predictive endpoint is the paired, date-averaged log-loss difference against the prior. Accuracy,
balanced accuracy, class F1 and multiclass Brier score explain different aspects of the result. A
correct class prediction and a profitable trade are separate events, so no accuracy threshold is
used as a proof of alpha.
The second question is whether a hybrid adds net economic value over its components and
comparable simple policies when every strategy must finance its holdings through one account.
The primary economic endpoint is the daily net-return difference from a costed, risk-constrained
equal-weightaccountonmatchingdates. Wedistinguishthisaccountfromadescriptivefrictionless
SPY reference. The third question concerns where errors occur: classes, instruments, calendar
blocks, observed volatility conditions, disagreement between experts and confident mistakes.
Such conditional descriptions identify hypotheses; they do not by themselves establish a causal
mechanism.
The fourth question is sensitivity to features, calibration, gate construction, training history,
refit cadence and execution assumptions. Controlled comparisons change a specified factor while
preserving the eligible information set and outcome definition. The fifth question concerns
operation: whether the same policy can run from fresh inputs with persistent capital, restart
after faults, refuse invalid state and produce durable evidence on its registered schedule. These
questions define separate engineering, historical-evidence, prospective-operation and manuscript
decisions. Broker readiness is a further decision outside the authorized scope.
2 Related work and the scope of comparison
Machine learning can model nonlinear conditional relationships in asset returns. Gu, Kelly and
Xiu compare a broad range of methods for risk-premium prediction and examine the importance
of interactions [1]. Their result motivates careful predictive comparisons; it does not supply
a performance target for this project. Their universe, prediction horizon, information set and
portfolio construction differ from our small daily classification study. We do not copy their
reported economic gains into a benchmark table or interpret a different task as a replication.
A simple trend rule is a demanding control for a more elaborate trading architecture because
it can capture persistent price behavior without learning a high-dimensional decision boundary.

Time-series momentum has been studied across futures markets [2]. Our rule combines a
trailing price-average condition with intermediate-horizon momentum on the existing equity/ETF
universe. It is long-only and financed through the same constrained local ledger as the ML policy.
It is therefore a project control inspired by trend-following, not a reproduction of the published
futures strategy.
Gradient boosting provides a flexible additive model built through successive loss reduction [3].
We pair a shallow histogram boosting classifier with regularized multinomial logistic regression
to contrast nonlinear and linear decision surfaces. The logistic model is both a component
and a useful simplicity control. Fixed capacity and chronological fitting make the comparison
interpretable. Increasing model count or training duration after seeing poor outer-period results
would change the scientific question and enlarge the search history; it would not repair a failed
hypothesis.
Probability forecasts require more than class ranking. Proper scoring rules reward distributions
that represent the forecaster’s uncertainty [4]. Log loss penalizes confident wrong answers strongly,
whereas accuracy only considers the largest probability. The Brier score measures squared error
across the complete class distribution. Temperature scaling is a low-dimensional way to alter
sharpness while preserving each row’s ordering [5]. Evidence from other application domains
does not imply that temperature scaling must improve financial forecasts. We therefore compare
raw and calibrated mixtures on identical outer observations and disclose the samples used to fit
the temperature.
Dependent observations complicate uncertainty. Adjacent forward-return labels overlap, and
symbols respond to common market shocks. Resampling instrument-date rows independently
would overstate the effective sample size. Block resampling follows the general motivation of
dependent-data bootstrap methods [6], while HAC estimators provide a complementary approach
for autocorrelated averages [7]. Our implementation preserves the entire cross section within a
sampled date and examines several block lengths. The assumptions remain approximate when
the return-generating process shifts over time.
Multiple testing is especially consequential in strategy research. Financial factor evidence
illustrates how an extensive search can make conventional single-test thresholds misleading
[8]. Holm adjustment controls a declared family of comparisons [9], but no adjustment can
recover undocumented researcher choices. The Deflated Sharpe Ratio and probability-of-backtest-
overfitting literature address aspects of selection risk [10, 11]. We do not report those estimators
here because a complete comparable trial history and their required assumptions are not available.
The current registry makes the new comparison family explicit; it does not erase earlier exposure.
Covariance shrinkage is motivated by the instability of estimated risk matrices [12]. Our risk
rule uses a fixed blend of sample covariance and its diagonal. This is a transparent regularization
assumption, not an implementation of the cited paper’s optimal shrinkage estimator. Exchange
timing follows the official NYSE calendar [13]. The operational design also recognizes that a
scheduled workflow file does not guarantee execution; workflow failures require direct evidence
[14]. These references establish methods and constraints. All empirical performance claims below
come from this project’s saved or newly executed experiments.

The verified market panel contains 55,242 instrument-session observations on 2,511 common
market dates. There are 686 recorded decision dates, 680 with mature outcomes, and 132
instrument-date predictions whose outcomes are unavailable at the historical cutoff. The inherited
release reports 726 refits. The inventory rechecked 722 preserved files and directly compared 66

saved reproduction outputs. These are new integrity checks of saved records, not a claim that all
old models were trained again.
The universe was inherited from the project rather than sampled from historical index
constituents. It mixes individual surviving companies, broad-market funds, sector funds and
alternative-asset funds. Economic exposures overlap: holding a growth stock and a fund that
contains it does not create two independent bets. The declared growth-group limit captures only
one part of that overlap. We cannot estimate delisting bias from this universe, and we do not
interpret the results as a market-wide stock-selection claim.
Raw provider OHLCV and adjusted series are separate artifacts. The term raw refers to
the provider’s returned fields before this pipeline’s adjustment transformation; it does not
certify an archival, unadjusted exchange tape. Adjusted prices incorporate the vendor’s present
interpretation of splits and distributions. Each snapshot records retrieval time and hashes.
Historical availability uses an explicit completed-session convention because the provider does not
supply an immutable historical arrival timestamp for every bar. A current-vintage reconstruction
is not equivalent to a point-in-time archive.
For the local simulation, quantities are total-return proxy units. Future provider revisions
can rescale historical adjusted prices, so daily marks chain the newly observed adjusted return
onto stored marks. That convention avoids treating a uniform rescaling as a windfall or loss in
existing holdings. It does not implement broker shares, dividend cash payments, fractional-share
restrictions or corporate-action entitlements. Raw responses and available action events are
retained for provenance, while broker accounting remains a separate unimplemented contract.

3.2 Admission, missingness and labels
Every admitted price panel must have unique, monotonically ordered session keys, finite positive
OHLC values, valid high/low ranges and positive observed volume. The exchange calendar,
rather than a generic weekday index, defines expected sessions. There is no holiday padding,
synthetic zero-volume bar, stale-price forward fill or silent fallback to the last successful forecast.
An unavailable bar stops the applicable batch before account decisions. Dropping a record,
skipping an event and predicting FLAT are different states and remain distinguishable in the
evidence.
For information observed at the end of session t, the first modeled fill is the next completed
close. The target return is therefore:

A return below -1% is DOWN, above +1% is UP, and an observed return between those

at the tail of the study, where decisions outnumber mature labels, and at partition boundaries,
where adjacent labels can otherwise reveal later evaluation prices.
Dataset accounting is performed separately for price rows, usable feature rows, decision rows,
label-complete instrument-dates and account sessions. A portfolio needs marks even when an
outcome label is not yet mature. Thus the last account sessions legitimately contribute to equity
measurement while being excluded from classification. The tables and claim map preserve those
denominators instead of calling every row a sample with independent statistical information.
3.3 Exposure log and optional information
Allhistoricaldatesinspectedinthepriortasksorsavedreleasearelistedintheevaluation-exposure
log. The old development, selection and audit labels are retained as names for reproducible
date ranges, not as a claim that the current analyst is blind to them. The new controlled
experiments use development-era chronological blocks and do not replace the previously selected
prospective shadow. Historical results are useful for diagnosing a design and measuring its
behavior; independent confirmation must come from later observations under a frozen rule.
Macro, news and crypto are excluded from the primary information set. A working public
endpoint does not establish historical vintage eligibility. Revised macro observations need
release/vintage timestamps; news requires retained content, version and availability times; a
bounded recent crypto endpoint does not supply the requested long archive. Legacy XGB,
LGBM, LSTM, MoE and zoo outputs remain preserved. Their different targets, information sets
and splits prevent treating their old headline scores as controlled architectural comparisons. The
registry records scope exclusions rather than silently dropping weak models.

The temperature is selected only from its registered candidate set using eligible past calibration

quality rather than creating a different argmax classification. The training-frequency control
uses Laplace-smoothed class counts from the fitting sample. Always-UP is a directional control;

The sealed release calibrated temperature and estimated gate benefit on the same trailing

temperature on its fitting block. The follow-up separates fitting, temperature selection and gate

advantage survives a distinct eligible block. It does not assume that separating the blocks must
improve returns; fewer calibration or gate observations may increase estimation variance.

At a refit decision, eligible labels are determined by their actual session endpoints. Fitting,
calibration and gate partitions are chronological. Purging removes any earlier-partition target
whose outcome reaches the next partition’s first decision. This rule is derived from execution
timing rather than a conveniently chosen gap. A transform learned from a preceding partition
is applied to later partitions without refitting. All fold records retain first/last dates, label
endpoints, sample counts and source/configuration hashes.
The controlled outer dates comprise four disjoint blocks, each containing the first 63 sessions
of a development half-year. Date-block resampling stays within each outer block. The bounded
experiment design varies one registered factor at a time. There is no Cartesian hyperparameter
search. If an inner choice is fitted, that choice is made entirely before its outer block; outer
results never select a replacement pilot policy. Training-history and refit-cadence experiments
align features, targets and evaluation dates, preventing a model trained on a different information
set from masquerading as evidence about architecture. Additional seeds cover six representative
instruments; they do not cover every instrument. Registered seeds characterize estimator
variability separately from uncertainty across dates. Seeds and correlated instruments are not
independent replications of the market.
The operational contract has an explicit exchange-session refit anchor and an explicit startup
model. Research replay and the daily runner use the same anchor. Weekly target changes
occur on the last eligible exchange session of the week, including holiday weeks, with an initial
allocation decision. Other sessions process pending fills, marks and drawdown controls without
routine rebalancing. These v2 rules are versioned changes; they do not retroactively alter the
sealed experiment’s dates or Friday-only results.

4.3 From probabilities to constrained targets
Expected target return is the probability-weighted mean of the fitting sample’s class-conditional
target returns. A positive prediction only requests an allocation when this estimate exceeds
the registered round-trip cost hurdle. The trend intent requires a positive price/long-average
ratio and positive intermediate-horizon momentum. A fixed hybrid combines the two intents;
the adaptive hybrid allows the ML contribution only when the past-data gate accepts it. The
forecasting mixture and the trading hybrid are distinct objects and are named separately in the
result tables.
Before account submission, target weights pass long-only, per-instrument, gross and group
caps. Trailing covariance is estimated from eligible observed returns, annualized, and shrunk
halfway toward its diagonal. If estimated target volatility exceeds the registered cap, all risky
target weights are scaled down proportionally. This rule does not guarantee future volatility or
drawdown, and estimation error remains visible in sensitivity tests. Idle cash earns zero under
the simulated account contract.
Orders represent changes from current holdings toward target holdings, not repeated purchases
for repeated UP signals. Pending buys reserve cash; pending sales are not spendable buying
power. Orders can be rejected when a price gap exceeds the reserved budget or projected exposure
violates limits. A drawdown halt requests reductions and persists through restart. Marked
market drift can temporarily breach a target cap between decisions, so the system records a
breach and blocks risk-increasing orders rather than pretending a target limit is a pathwise
guarantee.

4.4 Persisted decisions and operational stages
The collector validates release, dependencies and state before inference. A decision plan records
session, information cutoff, data manifest, model versions, probabilities, targets and marks before
account processing. The plan is immutable for that session. Account commits and final receipts
are recoverable stages: a restart resumes the same plan and uses event IDs to avoid duplicate
fills. Concurrent CLI, dashboard and scheduler requests share the same lock and state owner.
Release/model storage is separate from mutable account state. Backups retain original
receipts and database state, and restoration is checked at the recorded original path after moving
the original state out of the way. Relocating embedded absolute artifact paths is outside the
supported recovery contract. A stale writer, truncated file, missing model or changed source
must cause a visible halt; a success-shaped fallback would contaminate both the account and the
research dataset. Changes to the collector after qualification require a new release and evidence.
The old source and sealed releases are preserved as rollback and historical reproduction assets.

The primary predictive comparison averages each day’s candidate-minus-prior log-loss differ-
ence across symbols, then resamples contiguous date blocks. Keeping the cross section intact
avoids treating a market-wide error as many independent surprises. Circular wrapping allows
every date to start a block, but short samples provide limited distinct market episodes. Block-
length sensitivity examines a label-horizon-scale block, the registered primary block and a longer
block. Confidence intervals describe the behavior of this historical sample under the resampling
assumptions; they do not make the consumed audit confirmatory.
Economic comparisons use identically indexed daily net returns. Rebased charts make date-
range returns visually comparable but do not reset account holdings. Where a fresh-capital replay
is reported, it explicitly begins a new account and is separately labeled. CAGR compounds the
observed return over its annualized duration. The new portfolio diagnostics use a zero risk-free
rate for Sharpe and a zero minimum acceptable return for Sortino, consistent with zero-yield cash.
The sealed release separately reports Sharpe relative to a fixed annual hurdle; those differently
defined values are never interchanged. Sortino uses downside deviation relative to its disclosed
target. Undefined zero-variance statistics remain undefined rather than being converted into
evidence for a strategy.
Hypothesis families come from registered contrasts. Holm-adjusted values apply separately to
the specified predictive and economic families. Additional class, symbol and regime slices are
descriptive unless entered into a distinct family before computation. This avoids presenting a
favorable post hoc slice as a new independent experiment. Effect sizes and intervals take prece-
dence over binary significance language. Approximate minimum-detectable-effect calculations
use the estimated sampling variability; they are design aids, not guarantees of future power.
Execution robustness perturbs clearly named assumptions. Cost changes can retain decisions
while changing financing and fills. Delaying execution or restricting fill size may alter exposure
and subsequent account behavior. Missing-bar tests are admission/recovery tests and must fail
before decisions rather than interpolate a fictional observation. Risk-parameter changes create a
different target policy and are classified accordingly. The table records which components remain
frozen in each experiment so that a changed return is not assigned to the wrong cause.

Runtime evidence is separate from correctness evidence. Measurements disclose hardware,
interpreter, thread limits, sample counts and cold/warm conditions. Quantiles from small samples
are descriptive order statistics, not reliable tail service-level estimates. Compute budgets are
frozen after a development-only pilot, attempted trials retain status and errors, and cache keys
include data, source, configuration and fold boundaries. Exact repetition from saved source is
a numerical reproduction claim; operation across a genuinely new market period is a different
claim.
6 Prediction quality and error structure
The recent audit has 1,056 labeled instrument-dates across 48 sessions. Hybrid accuracy is
37.59%, while prior accuracy is 40.62%. Hybrid balanced accuracy is 37.24% and macro F1 is
0.372. The prior’s stronger aggregate accuracy does not imply equal recall across classes; the
class report makes that tradeoff visible. Log loss also favors the prior in this sample, so there is
no probability-quality improvement to hide behind a change in class balance.

Per-class reporting prevents a common majority class from concealing weak recall elsewhere.

observed examples of that class it recovered. F1 combines these two rates, and support states
the denominator. Row-normalized confusion matrices emphasize which true classes are confused,
while raw counts identify where most mistakes occur. The dominant transition can change when
class prevalence changes even if the conditional error rates remain stable.

11 Discussion and limitations
The audit probability comparison has Holm-adjusted p=0.980 in its registered diagnostic family.
The interval crosses zero, and the point estimate favors the prior. This combination supports a
bounded statement: the current hybrid has not established better probability forecasts on the
consumed audit. It does not prove exact equivalence, nor does it justify selecting a favorable
instrument after inspecting the heatmap. The full registry retains the controlled follow-ups
so that any narrower improvement is interpreted within its own dates, information set and
uncertainty.
The most useful scientific distinction is between a weak forecasting result and an invalid
experiment. Weak predictive or economic results remain publishable evidence when their
design, uncertainty and limits are explicit. Conversely, stronger reported performance would not
compensate for unavailable labels, inconsistent capital accounting or a search procedure hidden
from the reader. This investigation makes those dependencies visible and creates a path for
prospective replication without requiring the model to win.
Several limitations remain structural. The inherited universe has selection and survivorship
bias. Current-vintage vendor adjustments do not reconstruct exactly what a historical participant
saw. Overlapping ETFs and common market returns limit the independence of the cross section.
Execution-aligned labels overlap in time. The short recent audit and finite development folds
provide limited evidence across distinct regimes. Bootstrap intervals rely on local dependence
assumptions and do not establish robustness to every structural break.
The controlled training budget is intentionally bounded. It evaluates a family of fixed,
interpretable models rather than exhaustively searching all architectures. Excluding larger
legacy models from a controlled table avoids an invalid comparison; it does not prove those
models cannot work. A later architecture study would need its own registered information set,
chronological partitions, compute budget and independent confirmation. Adding news or macro
data would require actual availability evidence before any claim of incremental benefit.
Operational qualification also has a narrow scope. A local scheduler, persistent account and
successful recovery test make collection feasible, but the user’s host remains an availability
dependency. Synthetic fault injection does not reproduce every filesystem or power-loss condition.
The total-return proxy ledger omits real broker entitlements and market microstructure. No
external orders are enabled, and no result here should be read as authorization for them. The
deployment account remains cash unless independent evidence and separate execution gates
eventually support a change.
12 Conclusions
The project now has a reproducible historical investigation and a separately versioned path
for collecting prospective local evidence. On the recent audit, the calibrated mixture’s 1.0882
log loss does not beat the prior’s 1.0756; the capital-matched hybrid account also trails its
simple investable control. Engineering tests and a real scheduler trigger support the tested
collection contract, while sustained unattended sessions and final outcome maturity remain
unobserved. The historical manuscript reports the available negative and mixed findings; the
complete prospective study remains interim. No profitable-trader or broker-readiness approval
follows from this work.
